Released into Early Access in July 2026 [on Steam](https://store.steampowered.com/app/4134600/EverRail/) as well as [on Epic Game Store](https://store.epicgames.com/p/everrail-2567bc).

It's a challenging survival crafting game for up to 4 players.


As part of the programming team at [Aesir Interactive](https://aesir-interactive.com/), I am working on multiplayer topics and core features like: inventory system, dungeon spawning and the glider.

I also integrated achievements and DLC feature unlocks for both storefronts. For Epic Game Store, I used [Epic Online Services (EOS)](https://onlineservices.epicgames.com/) the new backend infrastructure that is set to replace Online Subsystem (OSSv1).
